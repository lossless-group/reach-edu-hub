## Why Care?

GitHub's first scan of this repo found 75 open alerts (2 critical). They
were real, but the larger problem was the lockfile. When `package.json`
moved to Astro 7, the lockfile was never regenerated and still pinned Astro
6.2.2 and @dididecks/shell 0.1.0. Vercel installs with
`--frozen-lockfile`, so every deploy since then has failed.

***

## What's New?

- **The lockfile is rebuilt from scratch** on Astro 7.4.13 and current,
  patched versions of everything beneath it (tar, vite, devalue, fast-uri,
  brace-expansion, js-yaml, sharp and the rest). `pnpm audit` is clean.
- **Two pnpm overrides** cover what upstream still pins: `image-size` to
  2.x (pptxgenjs declares it but loads `sizeof` instead) and the
  `path-to-regexp` inside `@vercel/routing-utils` to 6.3.0. Both are in
  `pnpm-workspace.yaml` for pnpm 12 and in `package.json` for older pnpm,
  which ignores the workspace file here.
- **`esbuild` is a direct dependency now.** @dididecks/shell imports it,
  but Vite 8 bundles with Rolldown and no longer brings esbuild along, so the
  build failed without it.
- **The `packageManager` pin is removed.** With a pin, pnpm 12 writes an
  extra document into the lockfile that pnpm 9 and 10 can't read, and Vercel
  may use either of those. Locally everything runs on pnpm 12, and Vercel
  picks its own version.
- **`pnpm-workspace.yaml` carries the settings pnpm 12 reads** (hoisting,
  peers, build approvals for esbuild and sharp). `.npmrc` keeps them for
  older pnpm, plus the registry mapping and the token for the private shell
  package.

Checked: frozen installs pass on pnpm 9, 10 and 12, the build completes,
and the dev server serves the gate page. Every route redirects to it, as the
auth middleware intends.
