## Pre-RAG synthesis

* [Fundraising Story 01 — The Apprenticeship Degree Is a New Category](pre-rag-synthesis/01-the-apprenticeship-degree-is-a-new-category.md) - Reach didn't build a better program; it built a new category of higher education — the work-embedded, debt-free, employer-paid degree — and the field's biggest funders are converging on the idea right as Reach has the only proven national instance of it. Fund the category-definer.
* [Fundraising Story 02 — Teaching Already Proved It](pre-rag-synthesis/02-teaching-already-proved-it.md) - Reach's apprenticeship degree isn't a pilot hoping to work; it already solved the teacher-shortage problem at scale — 420+ K-12 systems across 8+ states, debt-free, paid-while-you-learn. For education funders this is the rare 'fund the proven thing's next chapter' story.
* [Fundraising Story 03 — The Care-Economy Workforce Emergency](pre-rag-synthesis/03-the-care-economy-workforce-emergency.md) - Behavioral health and healthcare have an acute, quantified workforce shortage and a talent pool 'hidden in plain sight' — incumbent care workers who can't reach licensure. Reach's Apprenticeship College of Health turns those jobs into degrees. This is the corpus's hottest, best-funded, most urgent story.
* [Fundraising Story 04 — Economic Mobility Without the Debt](pre-rag-synthesis/04-economic-mobility-without-the-debt.md) - Nearly every funder in the corpus shares one north star: economic mobility. Reach's distinct claim is that it delivers measurable earnings gains in the learner's own community with zero debt — arguably the highest-ROI mobility intervention a funder can buy. This is the macro story that unifies the portfolio.
* [Fundraising Story 05 — Workers, Not Employers: Fixing the Broken Talent Marketplace](pre-rag-synthesis/05-workers-not-employers.md) - The talent marketplace is broken — millions of open jobs, millions of underemployed degree-holders, and employers who can't retain. Reach flips the model: it credentials the workers an employer already has, so talent development becomes retention. This story speaks to the workforce-development and skills-based-hiring funders.
* [Fundraising Story 06 — Talent Hidden in Plain Sight: Place-Based & Rural Pipelines](pre-rag-synthesis/06-place-based-and-rural-pipelines.md) - Reach's degrees don't require leaving town — learners earn them in their home communities, in the jobs they already hold. That makes Reach the natural vehicle for place-based and rural funders who want talent to stay and strengthen the local economy. This is the geographic cut across the portfolio.
* [Fundraising Story 07 — The AI-Era Learning Institution](pre-rag-synthesis/07-the-ai-era-learning-institution.md) - AI is reshaping the job pyramid into a diamond — hollowing out entry rungs and raising the bar for the rest. The durable answer isn't more front-loaded college; it's continuous, work-embedded learning. Reach is structurally the institution built for that world. This is the leading-edge, forward-looking story — and the one that ties Reach to Lossless's own AI work.

## Strategies

* [Pitch Narrative — Agent Workflow Maxxing: Operate Like a Team Five Times Your Size](strategies/agent-workflow-maxxing/pitch.md) - The internal pitch arc for the agent-workflow-maxxing strategy: the same catchy-line-plus-cited-numbers pattern as the funder decks, aimed at Reach/Lossless leadership. The case: a $25M campaign with an August 2026 anchor deadline and a funding landscape full of dated windows is exactly the workload a small team cannot cover manually — and agents are how it gets covered without abdicating judgment.
* [Pitch Narrative — NCAD Forge: Charter the Lab](strategies/agentic-product-lab/pitch.md) - The pitch arc for the agentic product lab (corpus slug ncad-forge): the Apprenticeship Degree runs on a technology stack nobody built, the people who understand it can now prototype it themselves, and the AI-in-education funding wave of 2025–26 is looking for exactly this kind of shared infrastructure. What's next, with your gift: a chartered, shared lab at NCAD — domain experts prototyping, engineers hardening, the whole member network compounding.
* [Pitch Narrative — Frontier Job Demand: The Job Is the Curriculum](strategies/frontier-job-demand/pitch.md) - The funder/employer-facing pitch arc for the frontier-job-demand strategy: course catalogs are lagging indicators, and the fields with the most unmet demand — advanced manufacturing, drones, applied AI — move faster than any curriculum committee. What's next, with your gift: the first apprenticeship degrees designed backward from frontier jobs, launched with the employers who are desperate for the talent.
* [Pitch Narrative — Foundational Capabilities, Measured Improvements](strategies/literacy-numeracy/pitch.md) - The funder-facing pitch arc for the literacy-numeracy strategy: the labor market pays for the skill, not the diploma — and most working adults are below the line on exactly the skills that pay. K-12 already proved what works and how to measure it; postsecondary never tried, because the degree assumes. What's next, with your gift: foundational-skills instruction woven into work and sized for the moments working adults actually have, measured continuously by audio-based AI — the first university delivering measured improvements in foundational capabilities, shared onward through NCAD, and compounding into the next generation.
* [Pitch Narrative — Reverse Engineer Available Funding: Fish Where the Whales Are](strategies/reverse-engineer-funding/pitch.md) - The internal pitch arc for the reverse-engineer-funding strategy: funding is a power law, the campaign's own anchors prove the method, and the mega-gifts research just handed the team a reverse-engineered map of every whale in Reach's waters. What's next: point the team's scarce hours at capacity × alignment, and size every ask to the largest amount that fits the funder's pattern.
* [Pitch Narrative — Rural Income Boosts: The Raise That Stays](strategies/rural-income/pitch.md) - The funder-facing pitch arc for the rural-income strategy: rural America's bind is a loop — shortages, low incomes, brain drain — and the Apprenticeship Degree breaks it at the hinge point of income, in place. Arkansas already proved it with a ~2.4 effect size. What's next, with your gift: the Delta expansion at the exact moment Walton re-aimed its home-region strategy at the same map.
* [Pitch Narrative — Upward Mobility: The Mobility Engine](strategies/upward-mobility/pitch.md) - The funder-facing pitch arc for the upward-mobility strategy: what's next, what could be — with your philanthropic gift. Builds on the CEO's June 2026 campaign deck ('what is') and extends it with the mobility evidence base and the funding-flows landscape. Catchy line per beat, KPIs underneath, every number cited by hex code.
* [Pitch Narrative — Workforce Development: Stop Training and Praying](strategies/workforce-development/pitch.md) - The funder-facing pitch arc for the workforce-development strategy: the system trains and prays; the evidence says exactly what works; the Apprenticeship Degree is the only model that stacks every proven design feature — and the corporate-philanthropy wave of 2026 is funding everything around it except the degree itself. What's next, with your gift: the degree-bearing backbone of American workforce development.
